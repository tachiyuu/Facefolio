Facefolio is a lightweight Facebook client for Android. It wraps the mobile Facebook website in a WebView and changes what it shows with scripts bundled in the app.

Each feature is a setting:
<ul>
    <li>Remove ads: hides sponsored posts and reels</li>
    <li>Show in feed: hide suggested posts, People you may know, AI-generated posts, Reels, Stories, Marketplace and group suggestions; a "N hidden · Show" line lets you see what was hidden</li>
    <li>Pinned tabs: choose which tabs appear in the top bar (Home is always shown; Groups can be added)</li>
    <li>Hide like and share counts</li>
    <li>Quiet badges: a dot instead of unread numbers</li>
    <li>Material You colours instead of Facebook's blues, readable in Facebook's light and dark themes</li>
    <li>AMOLED Black for Facebook's dark theme</li>
    <li>Sticky top bar, immersive mode, pinch to zoom</li>
    <li>Download media, copy images to the clipboard</li>
    <li>Desktop layout, switched on automatically in landscape and on large screens</li>
</ul>
External links open in your browser without Facebook's redirect and fbclid parameter. The app has no analytics of its own.
